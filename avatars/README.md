# Avatar assets

The per-video illustrations added in this update are configured Open Peeps variants generated with DiceBear 9.x. Original Open Peeps artwork by Pablo Stanley is licensed CC0 1.0; the bundled existing DiceBear license is retained in LICENSE.txt. Source: https://www.dicebear.com/styles/open-peeps/ and https://www.openpeeps.com/.

The illustrations are approximate, based on visible features in sampled video frames. They are not portraits or verified identities. `empty.svg` is a generic placeholder used for scene-only clips and weak matches.

## November 3 sampling

- `taiwan-1.svg`: Taiwan video around 10 seconds; short brown fringe, rectangular glasses, dark jacket.
- `germany-4.svg`: additional Germany video around 5 seconds; long straight brown hair and navy top.
- Argentina (opening, around 10, 60, and 112 seconds), Canada (opening, around 10, 45, and 80 seconds), Slovakia (opening, around 10, 35, and 42 seconds), and Singapore HDB (opening, around 15, 45, and 70 seconds): no clear speaker reference in sampled frames; retained `empty.svg`. This was frame sampling, not an exhaustive review of every frame.

The two new SVGs use the same DiceBear 9.x Open Peeps style, pale green background, and CC0 source artwork as the existing illustrations.
